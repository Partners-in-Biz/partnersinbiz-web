## Project Scope & Deliverables

This is a comprehensive project to build your digital foundation and then grow your direct booking revenue.

**1. Bespoke Website Development (Months 1-2)**
A visually stunning, user-friendly website that serves as the central hub for Saaiman Stays.
*   **Key Features:**
    *   Unique design aligned with the Saaiman Stays luxury brand.
    *   Detailed and beautiful property listings with high-resolution galleries.
    *   An integrated, seamless direct booking engine to maximize revenue.
    *   A mobile-first design for a perfect experience on any device.
    *   Analytics integration for data-driven decisions.

**2. Foundational SEO Sprint (Months 1-3)**
A targeted sprint to make your new website visible and discoverable on search engines from day one.
*   **Activities:**
    *   In-depth keyword research to target high-value guest searches.
    *   On-page SEO for all core pages (titles, descriptions, content).
    *   Technical SEO setup (sitemap, loading speed, mobile-friendliness).
    *   Local SEO foundation to attract guests searching for your locations.

**3. Google Business Profile Optimization (Month 1)**
We will claim, verify, and fully optimize your Google Business Profile to capture high-intent local searchers and appear prominently on Google Maps.

**4. Ongoing Growth (Month 4 onwards)**
Once the foundation is built, we shift to a continuous cycle of marketing, optimization, and growth, turning your website into a consistent source of direct bookings.
