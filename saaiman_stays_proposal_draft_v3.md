# Proposal for Digital Presence Overhaul: Saaiman & Saaiman Hospitality

**Prepared for:** Hendrik Saaiman  
**Date:** July 20, 2026

## 1. Executive Summary

This proposal outlines a comprehensive plan to establish a powerful, direct booking channel for Saaiman & Saaiman's hospitality business. Based on our analysis of your EMBA Capstone Strategy report, our primary objective is to create a digital presence that is not only aesthetically aligned with your brand but also a robust engine for growth, driving direct bookings and enhancing guest relationships.

We will deliver a complete solution encompassing a custom-built, brand-aligned website with an integrated booking system, a foundational Search Engine Optimization (SEO) sprint to ensure discoverability, and a fully optimized Google Business Profile to capture local and ready-to-book customers.

Our pricing is structured strategically to support your growth. We begin with a discounted introductory rate, which incrementally adjusts over nine months to align with the market-standard benchmark, allowing you to invest in your growth while managing cash flow effectively.

## 2. Project 1: Custom Hospitality Website

A premium, user-friendly website is the cornerstone of a successful direct booking strategy. It serves as your digital storefront, brand showcase, and primary sales channel, reducing reliance on third-party booking platforms and their associated fees.

**Deliverables:**

*   **Discovery & Strategy:** Collaborative workshop to define site architecture, user flow for bookings, and brand aesthetic.
*   **Custom Design:** A visually stunning, mobile-first design that reflects the unique brand and quality of Saaiman & Saaiman properties.
*   **Booking Engine Integration:** Seamless integration of a robust booking engine, allowing guests to view availability, select properties, and complete bookings directly on the site.
*   **Property Listings:** Beautifully crafted individual pages for each property, featuring high-resolution galleries, detailed descriptions, amenities, and maps.
*   **Content Management System (CMS):** An easy-to-use backend (e.g., WordPress or Webflow) empowering your team to update content, manage listings, and view booking information.
*   **Analytics & Reporting:** Integration with Google Analytics to track visitor behavior, booking conversions, and other key performance indicators.

## 3. Project 2: Foundational SEO Sprint

A beautiful website is only effective if potential guests can find it. This foundational SEO sprint is designed to make your new website visible to search engines and attract qualified traffic from day one.

**Deliverables:**

*   **Keyword Research:** Identifying the most valuable search terms potential guests use when looking for accommodations in your area.
*   **On-Page SEO:** Optimization of all website pages, including titles, meta descriptions, headers, and content, to align with target keywords.
*   **Technical SEO:** Ensuring the website is technically sound for search engines, including sitemap creation, robots.txt configuration, schema markup for hospitality businesses, and site speed optimization.
*   **Local SEO Foundation:** Building citations and ensuring NAP (Name, Address, Phone Number) consistency across the web to strengthen local search signals.

## 4. Project 3: Google Business Profile (GBP) Optimization

Your Google Business Profile is a critical asset for attracting local customers. It's often the first impression a potential guest has of your business in Google Search and Maps.

**Deliverables:**

*   **Profile Creation & Verification:** Full setup and verification of your GBP listing.
*   **Complete Optimization:** Meticulous optimization of all profile sections, including services, categories, photos, business description, and attributes, to maximize visibility.
*   **Initial Q&A Seeding:** Populating the Questions & Answers section with common queries to provide immediate value to visitors.
*   **Review Strategy Guide:** Providing a simple guide and template for encouraging positive guest reviews, a key ranking factor.
